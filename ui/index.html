Clipper

Videos

Turn long videos into Shorts

Paste a Kick, YouTube or Twitch link above (a past video, or a stream that's live right now), or add a video file from this PC. Clipper finds the strongest moments, reframes them to 9:16, adds captions, and shows a viral-chance score and a copyright risk check for each one.

Pick the type first: Podcast scores what's said (hot takes, stories, big laughs), while Gaming and IRL lean on loud reactions. A file that's already Short-length is kept whole and just scored and captioned.

Settings

Connected AI model

Only used for videos where you choose "Connected AI model" in the setup window. It reads the whole transcript to pick the moments and write titles. Needs an API key (except Ollama), and the transcript is sent to that provider.

Video quality
Your sounds and songs

Your own sound effects are matched to the moment by their file name (a "kaching" or "money" sound on money talk, "fail" or "bruh" sounds on fails, "huh"/"shock" sounds on surprises, booms and punches on the loud hits). The built-in sounds are generated by Clipper, so they're copyright-free.

Story card header (for the "Story card" framing)

Leave the name and handle empty to hide the header. Without a picture, the first letter of the name is shown in a circle.

Live streams

Paste the link of a stream that's live right now: Clipper records it and finds clips every 2 minutes while it runs.

One-time setup
  1. In Google Cloud Console, create a project and enable "YouTube Data API v3".
  2. Set up the OAuth consent screen and add your own Google account as a test user.
  3. Create credentials → OAuth client ID → type "Desktop app", and download the JSON file.
  4. Paste the file's contents below, save it, then press Connect and sign in.

Results

Real numbers from YouTube for the Shorts you uploaded with Clipper, next to the viral chance Clipper gave them. Once a video type has 8 public uploads older than 3 days, the built-in algorithm re-weights its signals toward what got views on your channel (half your results, half the defaults).

Music

Tracks you downloaded here plus anything you put in your own music folder.

Only tracks whose licence allows use in edited, monetised videos. Most need a credit in the description; Clipper adds it to the export file. A free licence doesn't stop an artist from also registering a track with Content ID, so an occasional claim is still possible.

These are today's most-played commercial songs. Clipper doesn't download them, because baking one into the video is what gets a Short claimed. To use one, export your Short with no song (or a quiet one) and add the sound inside the YouTube app when you upload, where it's licensed.
